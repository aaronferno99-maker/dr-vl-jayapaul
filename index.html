About

A teacher of speaking, first and always

His research asks one practical question: how do we get second-language learners to speak with confidence? The answers come from flipped classrooms, ICT tools, task-based lessons and counselling.

Dr. V.L. Jayapaul has taught at St. Joseph's College (Autonomous), Tiruchirappalli since 2001. He rose from Lecturer to Associate Professor and Head of the PG & Research Department of English, and now serves as Dean of the School of Languages and Culture. His work sits where English Language Teaching meets second-language acquisition.

He has guided Ph.D. and M.Phil. scholars to completion, serves on Boards of Studies and Academic Councils across Tamil Nadu, and is a regular resource person for faculty development programmes, bridge courses and soft-skills training.
